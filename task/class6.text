Here is a comprehensive overview of the full academic syllabus and curriculum for Class 6 based on the NCERT / CBSE framework.
1. Mathematics (गणित)
Number Operations & Theory: Knowing Our Numbers, Whole Numbers, Playing with Numbers.

Geometry & Shapes: Basic Geometrical Ideas, Understanding Elementary Shapes, Symmetry, Practical Geometry.

Arithmetic & Algebra: Integers, Fractions, Decimals, Ratio and Proportion, Introduction to Algebra.

Measurement & Data: Mensuration, Data Handling.
2. Science (विज्ञान)
Food & Nutrition: Sources of Food, Components of Food.

Materials & Chemistry: Sorting Materials into Groups, Separation of Substances, Changes Around Us.

The Living World: Getting to Know Plants, Body Movements, The Living Organisms and Their Surroundings.

Physics & Environment: Motion and Measurement of Distances, Light, Shadows and Reflections, Electricity and Circuits, Fun with Magnets, Air Around Us, Water, Garbage In, Garbage Out.
3. Social Science (सामाजिक विज्ञान)
History (Our Pasts - I): What, Where, How and When?, From Hunting-Gathering to Growing Food, In the Earliest Cities, What Books and Burials Tell Us, Kingdoms, Kings and an Early Republic, New Questions and Ideas, Ashoka the Emperor, Vital Villages & Thriving Towns, Traders, Kings and Pilgrims, New Empires and Kingdoms, Buildings, Paintings and Books.
Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat): The Earth in the Solar System, Globe: Latitudes and Longitudes, Motions of the Earth, Maps, Major Domains of the Earth, Major Landforms of the Earth, Our Country – India, India: Climate, Vegetation and Wildlife.
Political Science / Civics (Social and Political Life - I): Understanding Diversity, Diversity and Discrimination, What is Government?, Key Elements of a Democratic Government, Panchayati Raj, Rural and Urban Administration, Rural and Urban Livelihoods.
4. English
Literature & Supplementary:
Honeysuckle (Prose and Poetry units covering stories of nature, childhood, adventure, and inspiration).
A Pact with the Sun (Supplementary reader with moral and engaging stories).
Grammar & Writing Skills: Nouns, Pronouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs, Prepositions, Tenses, Articles, Subject-Verb Agreement, Notice Writing, Informal/Formal Letters, Paragraph Writing, and Reading Comprehension (Unseen Passages).
5. Hindi (हिंदी)
Vasant (वसंत भाग 1): पाठ्यपुस्तक की प्रमुख कविताएँ और कहानियाँ (जैसे—वह चिड़िया जो, बचपन, नादान दोस्त, पार नजर के, टिकट एल्बम, झाँसी की रानी आदि)।
Bal Ram Katha (बाल रामकथा): राम के जीवन पर आधारित कथा प्रसंग (अवधपुरी में राम, दो वरदान, राम का वन-गमन, लंका विजय आदि)।
Hindi Grammar (व्याकरण): संज्ञा, सर्वनाम, विशेषण, क्रिया, काल, कारक, लिंग, वचन, पर्यायवाची, विलोम शब्द, मुहावरे और अपठित गद्यांश।
