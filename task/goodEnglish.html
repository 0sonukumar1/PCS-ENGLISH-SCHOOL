Seasons in India (मुख्य ऋतुएँ)

Summer Season

ग्रीष्म ऋतु (गर्मी)
April to June | Hot weather, cold drinks & ice creams.

Monsoon / Rainy

वर्षा ऋतु (बरसात)
July to September | Raincoats, umbrellas & green trees.

Autumn Season

शरद ऋतु (पतझड़)
October to November | Leaves fall, pleasant cool air.

Winter Season

शीत ऋतु (सर्दी)
December to February | Warm clothes, fog & fireside warmth.

Spring Season

वसन्त ऋतु
March | Blooming flowers, beautiful nature & Holi.
