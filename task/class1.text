Here is the complete, comprehensive syllabus for **LKG (Lower Kindergarten)**, **UKG (Upper Kindergarten)**, and **Class 1**, structured according to modern foundational learning standards (aligned with the National Education Policy and NCERT guidelines):

---

## 1. LKG (Lower Kindergarten) Syllabus

*Focuses on early childhood care, play-based learning, basic motor skills, social interaction, and pre-literacy/pre-numeracy foundations.*

### A. English / Language Skills

* **Oral & Listening:** Recognizing simple sounds, reciting short rhymes, following basic instructions, and storytelling.
* **Pre-Writing Strokes:** Standing lines (`|`), sleeping lines (`-`), slanting lines (`/`, `\`), and curves (`(`, `)`).
* **Alphabet (A to Z):** Recognizing, saying, and writing uppercase letters (A–Z).
* **Vocabulary:** Picture reading, identifying common objects (fruits, animals, colors, shapes, body parts).

### B. Mathematics / Numeracy Skills

* **Pre-Math Concepts:** Big/Small, Tall/Short, Heavy/Light, Full/Empty, Inside/Outside.
* **Number Recognition:** Oral counting from 1 to 50; recognizing and writing numbers 1 to 20.
* **Shapes:** Recognizing basic shapes like Circle, Square, Triangle, and Rectangle.
* **Colors:** Identifying primary colors (Red, Blue, Yellow) and secondary colors.

### C. General Awareness & Environmental Concepts (EVS)

* **All About Me:** My name, age, gender, and parts of the body.
* **My Family & Home:** Parents, siblings, and rooms in a house.
* **Animals & Birds:** Domestic animals, wild animals, and common pet sounds.
* **Fruits & Vegetables:** Basic identification of common fruits and vegetables.
* **Good Habits:** Washing hands, brushing teeth, and keeping surroundings clean.

## 2. UKG (Upper Kindergarten) Syllabus

*Bridges play school and formal primary school by strengthening reading, writing, and early arithmetic skills.*

### A. English

* **Alphabet & Phonics:** Phonetic sounds of letters (A–Z) and blending simple two-letter/three-letter words (CVC words like *cat, bat, pin, pot, sun*).
* **Reading Practice:** Sight words, simple sentences (*"This is a ball," "The cat sat on a mat"*).
* **Grammar Basics:** Introduction to vowels and consonants (*A, E, I, O, U*), use of *A / An*, singular and plural forms, and opposites.
* **Writing & Composition:** Writing simple sentences about oneself ("My Self"), picture composition, and dictation.

### B. Mathematics

* **Number Work:** Oral counting up to 100; writing numbers 1 to 50; missing numbers, and backward counting (10 to 1).
* **Arithmetic Concepts:** Single-digit addition and subtraction using objects/pictures.
* **Before, After, Between:** Number concepts like "what comes before/after/between" (up to 20).
* **Measurement & Time:** Heavy vs. Light, Tall vs. Short, and basic days of the week.

### C. Hindi / Second Language (Oral & Basic Writing)

* **वर्णमाला (Alphabet):** स्वर (अ से अः) और व्यंजन (क से ज्ञ) की पहचान और लिखना।
* **शब्द ज्ञान:** दो, तीन और चार अक्षर वाले बिना मात्रा के शब्द (जैसे—कमल, घर, नल, कलश)।
* **मौखिक अभिव्यक्ति:** कविताएँ, कहानियाँ और चित्र वर्णन।

### D. General Awareness (EVS)

* **Community Helpers:** Teacher, doctor, policeman, postman, fireman, etc.
* **Transport & Traffic:** Land, water, and air transport; basic traffic light rules.
* **Seasons:** Summer, rainy, winter, and spring seasons.
* **Plants & Animals:** Parts of a plant, uses of plants, young ones of animals, and animal homes.

## 3. Class 1 Syllabus

*The entry point to formal primary education focusing on foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN).*

### A. Mathematics (*Joyful Mathematics*)

* **Pre-Number Concepts & Shapes:** Spatial understanding (near/far, top/bottom), 2D and 3D shapes.
* **Numbers (1 to 99):** Counting, reading, writing, place value (tens and ones), and comparison of numbers.
* **Operations:** Addition and subtraction up to 20 (and simple double-digit operations without regrouping).
* **Measurement & Data:** Measuring length using non-standard units (handspan, paces), heavier/lighter, capacity, handling simple data, and recognizing coins/notes.
* **Patterns & Time:** Identifying repeating patterns in shapes/colors and sequencing daily routine events.

### B. English (*Mridang* / Core Reader)

* **Reading & Comprehension:** Themed units on family, life around us, food, animals, and seasons. Reading stories, poems, and short passages.
* **Grammar & Vocabulary:**
* Naming words (nouns) and action words (verbs)
* Descriptive words (adjectives)
* Basic prepositions (*in, on, under, behind*)
* Use of capital letters and full stops


* **Creative Expression:** Guided sentence writing, drawing-based descriptions, and speaking about familiar topics.

### C. Hindi (*Sarangi* / Core Reader)

* **पाठ्यक्रम विषय:** परिवार, हमारा खान-पान, त्योहार और मेले, हरी-भरी दुनिया, और जीवन-जगत से जुड़े प्रसंग।
* **व्याकरण एवं लेखन:**
* मात्रा ज्ञान (आ, इ, ई, उ, ऊ, ए, ऐ, ओ, औ)
* संज्ञा और सर्वनाम के सरल रूप
* समान तुक वाले शब्द (Rhyming words)
* लिंग और वचन का प्राथमिक परिचय
* सुलेख और वाक्य रचना



### D. Environmental Studies / General Awareness (EVS)

* **Self & Family:** My body, sense organs, keeping clean, family members, and celebrations.
* **Food & Water:** Sources of food, healthy eating habits, and the importance of water.
* **Shelter & Clothing:** Types of houses, materials used, and clothes worn in different seasons.
* **Environment & Nature:** Plants, flowers, domestic/wild animals, birds, and insects.
* **Our Neighborhood:** School, market, hospital, community helpers, and national festivals.
