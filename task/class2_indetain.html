Subject 1: English Reader & Stories

Lesson 1: The Thirsty Crow (चतुर कौआ)

Once upon a time, there was a thirsty crow. He flew here and there in search of water. He saw a pitcher in a garden. There was very little water in it. He could not drink the water. He saw some pebbles nearby. He picked up the pebbles one by one and dropped them into the pitcher. The water level came up. The crow drank the water and flew away happily.

Moral: Where there is a will, there is a way. (जहाँ चाह, वहाँ राह।)

Subject 2: English Grammar Basics

1. Nouns (Naming Words)

A Noun is the name of a person, place, animal, or thing.

• Person: Rohan, Doctor | • Place: School, Bihar
• Animal: Dog, Elephant | • Thing: Pencil, Book
