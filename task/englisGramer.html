Chapter 1: The Sentence & Its Structure

A group of words arranged in a meaningful order to make complete sense is called a Sentence (वाक्य)।

Basic Structure of an English Sentence:
Subject (कर्ता) + Verb (क्रिया) + Object (कर्म) / Complement
Example 1: Rohan (Subject) plays (Verb) cricket (Object).
Example 2: The teacher (Subject) teaches (Verb) English (Object) in class (Complement).

Types of Sentences (वाक्य के प्रकार):

Sentence Type Meaning & Purpose Examples (सकारात्मक/नकारात्मक)
1. Assertive / Declarative साधारण कथन (Fact/State) • She goes to school daily.
• He does not like sweets.
2. Interrogative प्रश्न पूछने वाले वाक्य • Are you coming today?
• What is your name?
3. Imperative आदेश, सलाह या प्रार्थना • Please bring a glass of water.
• Don't run on the road.
4. Exclamatory अचानक उठी भावना/आश्चर्य • What a great surprise!
• Hurrah! We won the match.
5. Optative इच्छा, आशीर्वाद या प्रार्थना • May God bless you!
• Long live PCS English School!

Chapter 2: Parts of Speech Overview (शब्द भेद)

अंग्रेजी भाषा के सभी शब्दों को उनके कार्य के अनुसार 8 प्रमुख भागों में विभाजित किया गया है:

  • 1. Noun (संज्ञा): Name of person, place, thing, or idea. Rahul, Bihar, Book, Honesty
  • 2. Pronoun (सर्वनाम): Replaces a noun to avoid repetition. He, She, It, They, We
  • 3. Adjective (विशेषण): Describes or qualifies a noun/pronoun. Tall, Intelligent, Red, Four
  • 4. Verb (क्रिया): Expresses action, state, or occurrence. Read, Write, Play, Is, Have
  • 5. Adverb (क्रियाविशेषण): Modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb. Slowly, Very, Well, Yesterday
  • 6. Preposition (संबंध सूचक): Shows position or relation between words. In, On, At, Under, With
  • 7. Conjunction (समुच्चयबोधक): Connects words, phrases, or clauses. And, But, Because, Although
  • 8. Interjection (विस्मयादिबोधक): Expresses sudden emotions. Ouch!, Bravo!, Alas!, Wow!

Chapter 3: Noun In-Depth (Gender & Number Rules)

5 Kinds of Noun (संज्ञा के प्रकार):

  • 1. Proper Noun (व्यक्तिवाचक): Specific name of a person, place or thing. (India, Patna, Ganga)
  • 2. Common Noun (जातिवाचक): General name for same class. (Boy, Teacher, River, City)
  • 3. Collective Noun (समूहवाचक): Name of a group of entities. (Team, Class, Army, Crowd)
  • 4. Material Noun (द्रव्यवाचक): Substance from which things are made. (Gold, Silver, Milk, Water)
  • 5. Abstract Noun (भाववाचक): Quality, state, or feeling. (Wisdom, Childhood, Bravery)

Noun: Gender (लिंग)

Gender Category Definition Examples
Masculine Gender (पुल्लिंग) पुरुष जाति का बोध Boy, Man, Father, Lion, King
Feminine Gender (स्त्रीलिंग) स्त्री जाति का बोध Girl, Woman, Mother, Lioness, Queen
Common Gender (उभयलिंग) पुरुष एवं स्त्री दोनों के लिए Student, Doctor, Teacher, Child
Neuter Gender (नपुंसक लिंग) निर्जीव वस्तुएं एवं छोटे जीव Pen, Book, Table, Tree, Ant

Noun: Number Rules (एकवचन - बहुवचन)

Rule 1: अंत में -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x होने पर -es जोड़ें:
Box ➔ Boxes | Bus ➔ Buses | Church ➔ Churches | Class ➔ Classes

Rule 2: Consonant + Y होने पर 'Y' हटाकर -ies लगाएं:
City ➔ Cities | Baby ➔ Babies | Country ➔ Countries

Rule 3: Vowel + Y होने पर केवल -s लगाएं:
Boy ➔ Boys | Key ➔ Keys | Toy ➔ Toys

Chapter 4: Articles Mastery (A, An, The)

Articles qualify nouns and are divided into: Indefinite Articles (A, An) and Definite Article (The)।

Use of 'A' (एक): व्यंजन ध्वनि (Consonant Sound) से शुरू होने वाले एकवचन शब्दों से पहले।
Examples: A book, A pen, A doctor, A European (यु ध्वनि), A university (यु ध्वनि)।
Use of 'An' (एक): स्वर ध्वनि (Vowel Sound: अ, आ, इ, ई, उ, ए, ऐ, ओ...) से शुरू होने वाले शब्दों से पहले।
Examples: An apple, An elephant, An umbrella, An honest boy ('ह' साइलेंट), An M.L.A. ('ए' ध्वनि)।
Use of 'The': विशेष वस्तुओं, नदियों, पर्वतमालाओं, धार्मिक ग्रंथों, और अद्वितीय चीज़ों से पहले।
Examples: The Sun, The Moon, The Ganga, The Ramayana, The Taj Mahal, The first boy.

Chapter 6: Modal Auxiliaries (Modals का प्रयोग)

Modals सहायक क्रियाएँ हैं जो योग्यता, अनुमति, संभावना, और कर्तव्य व्यक्त करती हैं:

  • Can (क्षमता/योग्य): I can speak English. / He can lift this heavy box.
  • Could (भूतकाल की क्षमता): She could run fast in her childhood.
  • May (अनुमति/संभावना): May I come in, sir? / It may rain today.
  • Should (सलाह/कर्तव्य): You should respect your teachers. / We should help the poor.
  • Must (अनिवार्यता/पक्का): You must follow the traffic rules.
  • Would (विनम्र निवेदन): Would you like a cup of tea?

Chapter 7: All 12 Tenses Master Chart (काल के सभी 12 भेद)

Tense क्रिया के उस रूप को कहते हैं जिससे कार्य के समय का बोध होता है।

Tense Name Structure / Formula Affirmative & Negative Examples
1. Simple Present Sub + V1 (s/es) + Obj Rohan plays football. / He does not play.
2. Present Continuous Sub + is/am/are + V1+ing + Obj She is writing a letter. / She is not writing.
3. Present Perfect Sub + has/have + V3 + Obj I have completed my work.
4. Present Perfect Cont. Sub + has/have + been + V1+ing + for/since He has been reading since morning.
5. Simple Past Sub + V2 + Obj She cooked delicious food yesterday.
6. Past Continuous Sub + was/were + V1+ing + Obj They were playing cricket in the park.
7. Past Perfect Sub + had + V3 + Obj The train had left before we reached.
8. Past Perfect Cont. Sub + had + been + V1+ing + for/since She had been waiting for two hours.
9. Simple Future Sub + will/shall + V1 + Obj I will visit Delhi next month.
10. Future Continuous Sub + will be + V1+ing + Obj They will be studying at this time.
11. Future Perfect Sub + will have + V3 + Obj She will have finished her exam by 5 PM.
12. Future Perfect Cont. Sub + will have been + V1+ing + for/since We will have been living here for 5 years.

Chapter 8: Active & Passive Voice

Voice बदलने के 5 स्वर्ण नियम:
1. Active के Object को Passive का Subject बनाएं।
2. काल (Tense) के अनुसार उचित Helping Verb लगाएं।
3. हमेशा मुख्य क्रिया का 3rd Form (V3 - Past Participle) प्रयोग करें।
4. नए Object से पहले साधारणतया 'by' लगाएं।
5. Active के Subject को Passive का Object बनाएं।

Tense-wise Voice Transformation Table:

Tense Active Voice Sentence Passive Voice Sentence
Simple Present She sings a song. A song is sung by her.
Present Continuous They are painting the wall. The wall is being painted by them.
Present Perfect Rohan has solved the sum. The sum has been solved by Rohan.
Simple Past The policeman caught the thief. The thief was caught by the policeman.
Simple Future My father will buy a new car. A new car will be bought by my father.

Chapter 9: Direct & Indirect Speech (Narration)

वक्ता के शब्दों को ज्यों का त्यों कहना Direct Speech और अपने शब्दों में सार बताना Indirect Speech कहलाता है।

Tense Change Rules (यदि Reporting Verb Past Tense में हो):
• Simple Present ➔ Simple Past (is/am/are ➔ was/were)
• Present Continuous ➔ Past Continuous
• Simple Past ➔ Past Perfect (did ➔ had + V3)
• Will/Shall ➔ Would/Should | Can ➔ Could | May ➔ Might

Word Change Table:

Direct Word Indirect Word Direct Example Sentence Indirect Transformed Sentence
Today That day He said, "I am busy today." He said that he was busy that day.
Tomorrow The next day She said, "I will come tomorrow." She said that she would come the next day.
Now Then Ram said, "I am reading now." Ram said that he was reading then.

Chapter 10: Prepositions & Conjunctions

Important Preposition Usages with Examples:

  • In vs At (स्थान के लिए): 'At' छोटे स्थान/निश्चित बिंदु के लिए, 'In' बड़े शहर/देश के लिए।
    Example: He lives at Haspura in Bihar.
  • Since vs For (समय के लिए): 'Since' निश्चित समय (Point of time) के लिए, 'For' अनिश्चित अवधि (Period of time) के लिए।
    Example: I have been living here since 2020. She has been reading for 3 hours.
  • Beside vs Besides: 'Beside' = बगल में, 'Besides' = के अतिरिक्त।
    Example: Come and sit beside me. Besides English, he knows Hindi.

Key Conjunctions (जोड़ने वाले शब्द):

  • And (और): Joins similar sentences. ➔ Ram and Shyam are good friends.
  • But (लेकिन): Shows contrast. ➔ He worked hard, but he failed.
  • Because (क्योंकि): Shows reason. ➔ I was absent because I was ill.
  • Although...Yet (यद्यपि...फिर भी): ➔ Although he is rich, yet he is humble.

Chapter 11: Common English Errors & Corrections

अक्सर विद्यार्थी निम्नलिखित वाक्यों को बोलने एवं लिखने में गलतियाँ करते हैं। सही और गलत रूप ध्यान से समझें:

Incorrect Sentence (गलत रूप) Correct Sentence (सही रूप) Explanation / Reason (कारण)
Incorrect He is elder than me. Correct He is elder to me. 'Elder' के साथ 'than' नहीं बल्कि 'to' लगता है।
Incorrect One of my friend is coming. Correct One of my friends is coming. 'One of' के बाद संज्ञा हमेशा Plural होती है।
Incorrect She doesn't knows the answer. Correct She doesn't know the answer. 'Does' आने के बाद Verb में 's/es' नहीं जुड़ता।
Incorrect I am knowing him very well. Correct I know him very well. 'Know' क्रिया का प्रयोग Continuous में नहीं होता।
Incorrect What is the time in your watch? Correct What is the time by your watch? घड़ी के समय के लिए 'by' Preposition का प्रयोग होता है।
